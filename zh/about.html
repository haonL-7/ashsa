President Haixia Yue

The hottest issue in academia these days is convergence. This flow is rapidly developing around the US and Europe. Convergence means creating new value by combining existing fields and areas.

Despite excellent sports scientists working mainly in Asia, there is no integrated academic organization that can promote exchange among sports scientists. There is an urgent need for the foundation of a new academic organization that will integrate and study the various fields of sports science.

The Asian Sports and Health Association will play a leading role in discovering sports-related issues in Asia.

Let's all go on to the future with new dreams and visions.
