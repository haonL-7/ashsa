About ASHA

Open, Inclusive, Pragmatic, Win-win

The Asian Sports and Health Association (ASHA) is an international academic organization dedicated to advancing sports science and health across Asia.

Guided by the principles of Openness, Inclusivity, Pragmatism, and Win-win cooperation, ASHA brings together scholars and experts from across Asia to promote academic exchange, research collaboration, and industry development.

President's Greeting

President's Greeting

The hottest issue in academia these days is convergence. This flow is rapidly developing around the US and Europe. Convergence means creating new value by combining existing fields and areas.

Despite excellent sports scientists working mainly in Asia, there is no integrated academic organization that can promote exchange among sports scientists. There is an urgent need for the foundation of a new academic organization that will integrate and study the various fields of sports science.

The Asian Sports and Health Association will play a leading role in discovering sports-related issues in Asia and encouraging research to provide the knowledge and information necessary for sports.

Let's all go on to the future with new dreams and visions.

President Haixia Yue
