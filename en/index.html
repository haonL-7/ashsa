About the Conference

Overview

Advancing sports health science across Asia through interdisciplinary collaboration

The ASHSA International Conference provides a high-level academic exchange platform for scholars, practitioners, and policymakers in sports science, exercise medicine, and health management across Asia.

The conference features keynote lectures, thematic symposia, poster sessions, and hands-on workshops covering exercise for health, scientific training methodologies, and sports-medicine integration.

Conference Themes

Themes

Six core areas in sports and health sciences

Exercise & Health Promotion

Exercise prescription, physical activity and chronic disease prevention, population-specific intervention strategies

Scientific Training

Athletic conditioning, youth fitness development, military fitness, performance assessment and training monitoring

Sports-Medicine Integration

Sports rehabilitation, nutrition interventions, exercise psychology, traditional physical practices and health

Big Data in Sports Health

Wearable devices, AI applications in exercise science, health management informatics platforms

Youth Sports & Health

School physical education reform, sport-education integration, youth fitness promotion and lifelong activity habits

International Collaboration

Cross-border research mechanisms, sports health industry standards, policy dialogue and academic networking
