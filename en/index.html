About the Conference

Advancing sports health science across Asia

The ASHSA International Conference provides a high-level academic exchange platform for scholars, practitioners, and policymakers in sports science, exercise medicine, and health management across Asia.

The conference features keynote lectures, thematic symposia, poster sessions, and practical workshops covering exercise for health, scientific training, and sports-medicine integration.

Conference Themes

Core areas in sports and health

🏃 Exercise & Health

Exercise prescription, physical activity & chronic disease prevention, intervention strategies

🔬 Scientific Training

Athletic conditioning, youth fitness, military fitness, performance assessment & monitoring

🏥 Sports-Medicine Integration

Sports rehabilitation, nutrition, psychology, traditional exercise & health

📊 Big Data in Sports Health

Wearable devices, AI applications, health management informatics

🌱 Youth Sports & Health

School physical education, sport-education integration, youth fitness promotion

🤝 International Cooperation

Cross-border collaboration, sports health industry, standards & policy
