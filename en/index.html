About the Conference

Overview

Advancing sports health science across Asia，促进跨学科学术交流与合作

Asian Sports Health Association国际会议旨在为亚洲地区体育科学、运动医学、健康管理领域的学者、从业者和政策制定者搭建高水平的学术交流平台。

The conference features keynote lectures, thematic symposia, poster sessions, and hands-on workshops covering exercise for health, scientific training methodologies, and sports-medicine integration.

Conference Themes

Themes

Six core areas in sports and health sciences

Exercise & Health Promotion

Exercise prescription, physical activity and chronic disease prevention, population-specific strategies

Scientific Training

Athletic conditioning, youth fitness, military fitness, performance assessment and monitoring

Sports-Medicine Integration

Sports rehabilitation, nutrition interventions, exercise psychology, traditional physical practices

Big Data in Sports Health

Wearable devices, AI applications in exercise science, health informatics platforms

Youth Sports & Health

School PE reform, sport-education integration, youth fitness promotion

International Collaboration

Cross-border research mechanisms, industry standards, policy dialogue and networking
